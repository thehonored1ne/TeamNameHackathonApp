##For Administrator & Department Chair Users
Administrators oversee the entire matching process to ensure subjects are assigned to the most qualified faculty members.  

Step 1: Access the Portal
Sign In: Open the application and log in with your administrative credentials.  

Profile Management: Update your administrative contact details in the Profile section.  

Step 2: Build the Teacher Database
Create Profiles: Add new faculty members or update existing records in the Teachers section.  

Bulk Upload: Use the Teacher Import tool to upload large lists of faculty data from an external file.  

Verify Expertise: Ensure teacher profiles contain detailed skills and backgrounds to help the AI calculate accurate match scores.  

Step 3: Setup Subjects and Schedules
Define Subjects: List all courses available for the term in the Subjects section.  

Set Requirements: Assign specific keywords or expertise requirements to each subject.  

Establish Times: Input the official days and times for each class to define the Schedules.  

Step 4: Generate Assignments
Calculate Matches: Run the automated matching logic to compare teacher expertise against subject requirements.  

Review Scores: View the Match Score for each proposed assignment to see how well the faculty member fits the course.  

Finalize Load: Confirm and approve the assignments to set the official teaching load.  

Step 5: Monitor Activity
Notifications: Check for alerts regarding assignment updates or system changes.  

Audit Logs: Review the Audit Log to see a chronological history of all administrative actions taken within the app.  

##For Teacher Users
Teachers manage their own professional information and view their final class schedules.  

Step 1: Access the Dashboard
Sign In: Log in using your assigned teacher account.  

Dashboard Overview: View your current status and any immediate alerts from the administration.  

Step 2: Manage Professional Details
Update Profile: Keep your contact information and professional background current in the Profile area.  

List Expertise: Describe your specific skills and experience; this ensures the system matches you with the right subjects.  

Step 3: Define Your Availability
Set Free Time: Navigate to the Availability tab.  

Select Slots: Mark the specific days and hours you are available to teach for the upcoming term.  

Step 4: Review Your Teaching Load
View Assignments: Once approved by the Chair, check your assigned subjects in the Assignments or Schedules section.  

Check Details: Review the assigned class times, rooms, and match scores for your courses.  

Step 5: Receive Updates
Alerts: Stay informed through Notifications if an administrator changes your schedule or profile.
